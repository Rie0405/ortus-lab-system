What the outputs mean

  • Available slots — (sealed × orders/stock) + orders left open.
  • ADU — Average Daily Usage in order slots. Quantity sold ÷ MA days.
  • Days remaining — Available order slots ÷ ADU.
  • ROP — Reorder Point in order slots. ADU × lead time.
  • Suggested restock — Order slots to cover the ROP gap; sealed packages = ceil(slots ÷ orders per stock).
  • Alert threshold — Max order-slot capacity × alert %.
  • Urgency — OUT / CRITICAL (within lead time) / LOW (at alert %) / NO_SIGNAL / OK.
  • Restock by / Last supply — Dates only (order-by date and last supplier date).
